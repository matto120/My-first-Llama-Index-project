General Workplace Procedures

All personnel must follow these procedures to maintain a safe and efficient working environment.

Access Control

  • Staff must display ID badges at all times.
  • Visitors require supervisor approval before entry.
  • Restricted zones are accessible only to authorised personnel.

Equipment Use

  • Inspect equipment before and after use.
  • Report faults immediately via the digital maintenance form.
  • Do not modify instrument settings without approval.

Data Handling

  • Store all digital files in the central repository.
  • Backups occur automatically every 12 hours.
  • Confidential data must be encrypted before transfer.
