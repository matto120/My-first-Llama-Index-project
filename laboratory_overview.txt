Aurora Biotech Laboratory – Overview

Aurora Biotech Laboratory was established in 2024 as a small-scale research facility focused on synthetic enzyme development and environmental microbiology.
The lab contains three primary work zones: the Wet Chemistry Bench, the Microbial Culture Suite, and the Instrumentation Bay.
All staff must complete the internal competency module before accessing restricted areas.
The laboratory’s mission is to produce reliable experimental data using controlled procedures and validated equipment.
