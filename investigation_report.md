# Investigation Report – Sample IR-22-07

**Date:** 14/08/2025  
**Investigator:** Dr. L. Harwood  
**Subject:** Unexpected turbidity in Batch C-19 nutrient solution

## Summary
During routine quality checks, Batch C-19 displayed turbidity inconsistent with previous production runs. Initial microscopy revealed particulate matter resembling cellulose fragments.

## Findings
- pH drifted from 7.0 to 8.1 over 48 hours.
- Conductivity increased by 12%.
- No microbial growth detected using standard plate assays.
- Filtration logs show a 3-minute pressure drop during processing.

## Conclusion
The turbidity likely resulted from partial filter membrane delamination. Batch C-19 is unsuitable for further use and must be discarded.

## Recommendations
- Replace membrane cartridges before next production cycle.
- Implement continuous pressure monitoring during filtration.
